\section{Background results admitted}\label{sec:app-facts}

This appendix lists what the paper takes from the folder \texttt{Lib} of the Lean text,
the part classed there as known in the literature. The first list contains the results
that the text states as Facts, without proof. A few of them are proved in the folder
\texttt{New} but are known results; the text says so where it states them. The second
list contains every other declaration of \texttt{Lib} that the text cites, in the
course of a proof or of an explanation, without stating it as a Fact. Both lists are
generated from the source of the paper, so together they are complete. The statements
are those of the Lean text, which are often weaker than the theorems of the
literature; the paper has not compared them with their sources.
